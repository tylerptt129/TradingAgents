## Description
Turn saving money into a game! SaveQuest helps you build healthy financial habits through fun, structured savings challenges.

**HOW IT WORKS**
Pick a challenge, log your daily savings, and watch your progress grow. Earn badges, build streaks, and crush your savings goals one day at a time.

**PRE-BUILT CHALLENGES**
- 52-Week Challenge — Save $1,378 by saving $1 more each week
- 30-Day No-Spend — Cut non-essential spending for a month
- Penny Challenge — Start with $0.01 and add a penny each day
- Round-Up Challenge — Save the change from every purchase (Premium)
- 100 Envelope Challenge — Random daily saves totaling $5,050 (Premium)

**FEATURES**
- Visual progress tracking with rings and calendars
- Daily streak counter to keep you motivated
- 15 achievement badges to unlock (common to legendary!)
- Beautiful dark-mode interface
- Works completely offline — your data stays on your device

**PREMIUM ($0.99 ONE-TIME)**
- Unlimited active challenges
- All premium challenges unlocked
- Advanced savings analytics
- All achievement badges
- Custom challenge creation
- Export savings data

No subscriptions. No ads. Pay once, yours forever.
