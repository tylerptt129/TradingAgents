### Description

Stride is the beautifully designed habit tracker that makes building better habits feel effortless. With a stunning dark UI, satisfying animations, and smart streak tracking, Stride turns your daily routines into achievements worth celebrating.

**Why Stride?**

- Beautiful dark interface with luminous gradient cards
- One-tap habit completion with satisfying visual feedback
- Smart streak tracking that understands your schedule
- GitHub-style heat map to visualize your consistency
- 21 achievement badges to unlock as you progress
- 25 curated habit presets to get started in seconds

**FREE FEATURES:**
- Track up to 3 active habits
- Daily tracking with streak counters
- Basic completion history
- 7-day overview
- Core achievement badges

**PREMIUM ($2.99 one-time):**
- Unlimited habits
- Detailed analytics & insights
- Weekly and monthly heat maps

- Advanced streak statistics

- Priority support
